# What's New in Version 1.19.2

**Released:** November 15, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Equity waits for the day to finish

The equity book now decides from the last finished daily bar. It will not buy and sell the same stock over and over while today's bar is still forming. One decision is kept for that finished day.

### Drawdown is the drop in the account

The performance page shows how far the account has fallen from its high in this paper window. A profitable stretch no longer prints a huge drawdown.

### A flat Kraken book is a valid reading

The Kraken view stays up when that book has no open position.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- This is still paper trading
- Daily loss and quiet-hour limits are unchanged
- No action is required
