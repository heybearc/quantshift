# What's New in Version 1.19.0

**Released:** November 6, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### A separate sprint paper book

Kraken Sprint is its own $5,000 paper account. It can take a 15-minute break when the target is at least 1%, and it can be short. It does not use the cash or the trades from Equity, Coinbase, or the existing Kraken book.

The dashboard, trades, performance, and monitoring pages list it on its own. Its estimated cost is the same 40 bp Kraken estimate, shown beside the result and not taken from the account.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The other three paper books are unchanged
- No live money is in use
- No action is required
