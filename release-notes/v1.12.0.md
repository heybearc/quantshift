# What's New in Version 1.12.0

**Released:** October 10, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### See why a trade was placed

Trades and positions now include the reason for each new fill: the strategy, the market condition, and the sentiment reading at that moment.

**What you can do:**
- Open Trades and read the Why column
- Open Positions and see the same note under the strategy

Older open trades stay blank until the next fill.

### Market condition panel shows real readings

The dashboard market panel now shows trend, volatility, and how much of the market is above its recent average. These come from the same reading the bot already uses.

**What you can do:**
- Switch to the crypto bot to see the current reading
- The equity reading appears when the stock market is open

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Open profit is included on the dashboard

The header total now includes profit and loss on positions that are still open.

### Notification and admin settings save

Saving notification preferences and admin settings now stores the values you chose.

### Bots keep their books after a restart

A restart matches the broker and the saved book again, and it no longer clears the book when the broker read comes back empty.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- Existing positions do not get a reason written backward
- The equity book is still limited while it is above the leverage cap
