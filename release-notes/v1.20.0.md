# What's New in Version 1.20.0

**Released:** November 19, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Connect your own broker

My book now has a catalog of brokers you can connect. Alpaca can be paper or live. Coinbase and Kraken use the live account. QuantShift checks the key, stores it, and shows your cash, equity, buying power, and holdings. You can send a market order from that account. A live order asks you to confirm it first.

**What you can do:**
- Save a key for Alpaca, Coinbase, or Kraken
- Refresh the balance
- Send a buy or sell
- Remove the connection

**How to use it:**
1. Open **My book** and choose **My brokers**
2. Pick a broker and choose **Connect**
3. Enter the key from that broker and save it

### Ask for a broker we do not list

Charles Schwab, Interactive Brokers, and Tradier are listed, and you can request the adapter. Any other broker can be submitted with its name, website, and API notes. That request does not store a key or send an order.

### A personal book beside the house books

You can keep your own paper book, mark symbols you want to trade, and try a published community strategy on that personal book.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Equity stays on the large-cap list

The equity book keeps scanning the large-cap list when the broker adds an exchange it does not know. It also waits out the longer daily history instead of stopping.

### One decision at a time

Kraken repeats the same open order instead of sending a second one. Each book keeps a single writer, so a restart does not double up.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- House mode is unchanged and still follows the shared paper books
- A connected live account can send a real order after you confirm it
- A broker request is a review item, not a connection
- No other action is required
