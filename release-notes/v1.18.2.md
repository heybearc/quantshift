# What's New in Version 1.18.2

**Released:** October 16, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### A stopped snap waits for a fresh setup

When a snap-back trade stops out, that coin is not taken again on the same side until price closes back through the middle of its recent range. The other side of the same coin can still be traded. A trade that reaches its target does not start this wait.
