# What's New in Version 1.13.0

**Released:** October 11, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Rejected accounts stay on record

Rejecting someone removes them from Pending Approvals and from the main Users list. Open Users and choose the Rejected tab to see who was turned away. That email address and username cannot register again.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Dashboard numbers match their pages

Trade counts, win rate, drawdown, open sessions, recent audit activity, and system health on the dashboard now match the pages those cards open.

### Approving or rejecting an account works

An account that is waiting for approval can be approved after the email is verified. Rejecting an account takes it off the queue instead of failing with no change.

### User status is easier to read

The Users page shows whether an account is active, waiting for approval, or inactive.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action is required for accounts that are already approved
- An account still has to verify its email before you can approve it
