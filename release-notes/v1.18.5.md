# What's New in Version 1.18.5

**Released:** October 20, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Estimated trading cost on the dashboard

The performance table now shows an estimated cost next to each result, and a result after that cost. The estimate is not taken from the account. Equity uses a small slippage allowance. Coinbase and Kraken use a percentage of each fill.

**What you can do:**
- See the paper result and the estimated cost on the same row
- Compare the result after that cost without changing the paper account

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The paper account is unchanged
- The cost is an estimate, not a charge
- Coinbase and Kraken still follow their current paper rules
