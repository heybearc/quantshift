# What's New in Version 1.15.1

**Released:** October 15, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Crypto results name the strategy that opened them

A closed crypto trade is stored under the strategy that opened it. A price that blinked flat is no longer counted as a loss.

### Crypto waits for the hour to finish

A new crypto signal uses a finished hour. A coin that moves with one the book already holds is skipped.

### Crypto paper cash stays put on a restart

Restarting the crypto bot no longer refills the paper account.

### Equity keeps going on a busy day

A busy day no longer stops the equity bot for the rest of the session. The market reading keeps updating after the close. Loss and drawdown limits still apply.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Paper trading is still on. This release does not turn on live capital
- Equity, Coinbase, and Kraken books were left as they were
- No other action is required
