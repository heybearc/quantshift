# What's New in Version 1.13.1

**Released:** October 12, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Market regime shows its real name

The dashboard names the current market regime, such as Bull Trending or Low Vol Range. Trend and volatility are filled in when there is enough price history to measure them.

### Kraken paper trading can read prices again

The Kraken paper account can load candle data and run a full cycle. It still places an order only when a strategy signals one. Polygon uses its current symbol.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action is required
- Kraken remains a paper account
- Equity buys can still be limited while the book is leveraged
