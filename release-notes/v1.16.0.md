# What's New in Version 1.16.0

**Released:** October 16, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Crypto and Kraken use one setup

Coinbase and Kraken paper trading now wait for a daily washout at a pivot, with the trend pointing the same way and room to the next level. Equity keeps its existing strategies.

Coinbase stays long only. Kraken may take the short side, without leverage. A coin already held is left until its own stop or target.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Paper trading is still on. This release does not turn on live capital
- These trades will be uncommon. Every daily trend light has to agree, and the next level has to pay at least twice the risk
- Equity, Coinbase, and Kraken books were left as they were
- No other action is required
