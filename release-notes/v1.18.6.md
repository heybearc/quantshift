## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Crypto and Kraken can look for a volatility break again

A chart timeframe was missing, so the volatility break never had a chance to fire. Coinbase and Kraken now see both the daily chart and the hourly chart. The washout and the band snap are unchanged.
