# What's New in Version 1.11.1

**Released:** October 10, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Crypto practice trades stay on the paper account

Practice orders for the crypto bot are simulated locally. They are no longer sent to the live Coinbase account.

**What this means for you:**
- Paper results stay separate from real money
- You can watch practice fills without moving live funds

---

### Coins Coinbase no longer lists were replaced

A few pairs the bot was watching had been removed from Coinbase, so those orders could not be placed.

**What changed:**
- Removed Maker, EOS, Render (old ticker), and Loopring
- Added Sui, Aptos, Render, and Celestia in their place

The bot can now request prices and place practice orders for the full watch list again.

---

### Equity book can hold the full 10 positions

The stock bot was stopping at 5 open positions even though the limit is set to 10.

**What this means for you:**
- New stock buys can continue until 10 positions are open
- The book fills only while the US market is open
- Existing positions were not reopened by hand

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- The dashboard looks the same
- Stock positions will grow toward 10 only after the market opens
