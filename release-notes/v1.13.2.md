# What's New in Version 1.13.2

**Released:** October 12, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Monitoring shows the live books

The monitoring page lists equity, crypto, and Kraken, with a recent heartbeat for each. Strategy results show the strategy name, the real win rate, and all-time results.

### Risk bars stay inside their cards

A book that is over its position cap still shows a full red bar, and the bar stops at the edge of the card. Portfolio heat is left blank when the open positions do not have stops, instead of treating a gain as a limit breach.

### Regime names are readable

The monitoring regime card says Bull Trending, Rising or Falling, and how volatility compares with its average. Breadth is the share of symbols above their 20-day average.
