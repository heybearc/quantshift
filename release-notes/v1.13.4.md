# What's New in Version 1.13.4

**Released:** October 13, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Equity sells stay long-only

An equity exit now closes only shares the account already holds. A sell while flat does nothing. An existing short is bought back instead of sold again. The stop stored at the broker is shown on the position.

### Regime page and drawdown

The regime page shows the latest equity reading. Drawdown on the monitoring page is a percent with two decimal places. The optimization page includes the Kraken paper strategy.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action is required
- Paper trading is still on. This release does not turn on live capital
