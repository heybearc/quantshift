# What's New in Version 1.14.0

**Released:** October 13, 2026

---

## ✨ New

### The bots study their own strategies

Once a day, each bot scores all of its strategies on a name it holds, or the next name it is allowed to trade. The strategy that held up better gets a little more of the book. The weakest gives a little up. No strategy can take the whole account from one lesson, and the risk per trade does not change.

A setting changes only when the later history beats the setting already in use.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Drawdown is the account, not the profit peak

The performance page was treating a small profit peak as the whole account, so a giveback of a few dollars could show as more than 200%. Drawdown is now the decline in account equity, shown without a plus sign.

### Equity stops on a resting bracket are stored

A filled equity buy keeps its protective stop on a held order. That stop is now saved on the position.
