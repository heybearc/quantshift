# What's New in Version 1.18.3

**Released:** October 16, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Stats stay current while you are signed in

Dashboard, performance, trades, positions, risk, and monitoring keep updating on their own. The numbers also refresh as soon as you come back to the tab. You do not need to reload the page.

- A stopped snap still waits for a fresh setup on that side
- Coinbase stays long only. Kraken may still short
