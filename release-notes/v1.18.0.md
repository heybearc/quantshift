# What's New in Version 1.18.0

**Released:** October 16, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Crypto and Kraken can trade a short snap-back

Coinbase and Kraken can now take a smaller trade when a 15-minute bar stretches to the edge of its recent range and closes back inside. The target is the middle of that range. If the trade is still open after three hours, it is closed.

The daily washout and the volatility squeeze are still there. Equity is unchanged.

Coinbase stays long only, and it skips a snap that is too small to clear the fee. Kraken may short the same setup, without leverage.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Paper trading is still on. This release does not turn on live capital
- These snap-back trades are labeled on their own, separate from the washout and the squeeze
- A coin that is already held is left until its own stop or target
- Coinbase may still be quiet. Kraken is the book more likely to take one of these trades
