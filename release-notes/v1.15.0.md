# What's New in Version 1.15.0

**Released:** October 14, 2026

---

## ✨ New

### Kraken can sell short

The Kraken paper book can open a short when a strategy says the price is stretched, and buy it back when the strategy says to cover. Equity and Coinbase stay long only. A short shows as Short on the positions page, with its stop above the entry and its target below.

### Kraken uses the full strategy set

Kraken now runs the same seven strategies as the other books. Each one can go long or short.

### The Kraken list refreshes

Kraken keeps a curated set of dollar pairs and rechecks which of them are still listed and still trading enough. Quiet or missing names drop off. The list is capped so the book stays small.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Take-profit stays on the position

A Kraken target was dropped when the bot saved the position, so a winner had a stop and no target. The target is saved, shown on the positions page, and restored if the bot restarts.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- The Kraken paper book was reset so the new shorts and targets start clean. Equity and Coinbase books were left as they were
- Paper trading is still on. This release does not turn on live capital
- No other action is required
