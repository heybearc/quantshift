# What's New in Version 1.17.0

**Released:** October 16, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Crypto and Kraken can trade a volatility squeeze

Coinbase and Kraken can now take a trade when price leaves a pivot while the bars are unusually quiet. The daily washout setup is still there. Equity is unchanged.

Coinbase stays long only. Kraken may short either setup, without leverage. A coin already held is left until its own stop or target.

### More room in the crypto and Kraken books

Coinbase may hold up to 12 coins. Kraken may hold up to 8. Cash limits still apply, so a full-size coin cannot fill the whole account.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Paper trading is still on. This release does not turn on live capital
- A squeeze trade still needs a finished hour and a pivot break
- Equity, Coinbase, and Kraken books were left as they were
- No other action is required
